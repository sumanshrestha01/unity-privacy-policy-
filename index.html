Privacy Policy: Scam Call Simulator 3D

Developer: Mad Axe Studios · Effective date: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Scam Call Simulator 3D ("the game") handles and why. The game does not require an account, and we do not operate our own game-data servers. The game uses Google AdMob and Google Firebase services, described below. Those services may automatically collect or process certain information even when you do not enter personal details.

1. Information we do not ask you to provide

We do not require your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise device location, or payment details to play. The game does not make real phone calls. Google services integrated into the game may nevertheless process device identifiers, network information, usage data, and approximate location inferred from an IP address as described in sections 3 and 4.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Player name: the name you type when the boss asks is saved on your device so characters can greet you. The game does not intentionally transmit this name to our servers.
  • Game progress and settings: your career, upgrades, scores, in-game notes, and preferences such as sound, vibration, and graphics are saved locally.

These gameplay records are intended to remain on your device. Removing the game or clearing its app storage generally deletes them from the device, subject to any Android system backup or restore behavior. Separately, Google services may process technical information about game use, as explained below.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve, measure, protect, and, where permitted, personalize ads, Google may process your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and interactions with advertisements. Ad personalization depends on applicable law, consent choices, and your device settings.

  • Where required, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present consent or privacy choices. Your choices are applied to advertising requests as required by the platform and applicable rules.
  • Where the game indicates that privacy options are available, you can revisit them under Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID through supported Android settings.

For more information, see How Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics, crash reports, notifications, and configuration (Google Firebase)

The game uses Google Firebase for game analytics, crash diagnostics, optional notifications, and configuration updates. Google processes technical information for these functions. We do not sell this information. While the game does not require your real name or account, identifiers collected by these services may still qualify as personal data under applicable privacy laws.

  • Firebase Analytics: usage events (such as sessions, screens, in-game days, completed calls, missions, and upgrades), device and app characteristics (for example model, Android version, game version, and language), an app-instance identifier, and potentially advertising identifiers and approximate geographic information derived from network information. We use aggregated and event-based reporting to improve gameplay and performance.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: errors and crash diagnostics, device and app details, an installation or crash-reporting identifier, and limited diagnostic game state (such as the current in-game day) when included in a report.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: a messaging registration token and related technical information needed to deliver occasional game news or event notifications. On Android 13 and later, notification display requires your permission. You can change notification permissions in Android settings.
  • Firebase Remote Config: a Firebase installation identifier and technical request information used to retrieve game settings and configuration from Google servers.

More information: Firebase privacy and security.

5. Links to other apps

The game may link to Google Play listings for apps by Mad Axe Studios, Veritas Studios, and other developers. When you follow an external link, the destination service's privacy policy applies.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network access: to load advertisements and privacy messages, retrieve configuration, and transmit technical analytics and crash diagnostics.
  • Notifications: to display optional game announcements where enabled and permitted by Android.
  • Vibration: for optional haptic feedback, controllable in game settings.
  • Advertising ID: where available and permitted, used by integrated advertising and measurement services.

7. Children and intended audience

The game is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The intended audience declared in Google Play Console and the game's advertising settings should reflect this age range. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the game or its integrated services, contact us using the address below so we can review the concern and assist where reasonably possible.

8. Security, retention, and deletion requests

Locally stored gameplay data generally remains on your device until you clear the game's storage or uninstall it, subject to Android backup behavior. Technical information processed by Google AdMob and Firebase is subject to the relevant services' retention and deletion mechanisms. Transmission to these services normally uses encrypted network connections.

For privacy questions or requests to access or delete information associated with your use of the game, email veritasstudios.support@gmail.com with the subject Scam Call Simulator 3D Privacy Request. Describe your request and, if available, provide relevant non-sensitive details that may help us identify applicable records. Please do not email passwords or sensitive identification documents.

We will review requests and explain what actions are available, including deletion or assistance with deletion where we have sufficient technical access and a lawful basis to act. Some SDK-generated data may not be directly identifiable or deletable by us without an associated identifier; Google's own retention and privacy controls may also apply. We will not claim to delete data we cannot access or control.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when game features, services, or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a revised policy takes effect.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: veritasstudios.support@gmail.com.
