Privacy Policy: Cold Call Chaos

Developer: Mad Axe Studios · Effective date: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Cold Call Chaos ("the game") handles, and why. We keep it simple: the game has no accounts, and we do not run any servers that collect your data.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or payment details. The game does not make real phone calls.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • Player name: the name you type when the boss asks is saved on your device so characters can greet you. It is never sent to us.
  • Game progress and settings: your career, upgrades, scores, notes you write in the in-game Notes app, and settings (sound, vibration, graphics) are saved locally.

This information stays on your phone. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, device and app information, IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this to show personalized ads, where permitted and where you have consented.

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK, and some US states), the game asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before showing personalized ads.
  • You can change your ad choices at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices (when shown in your region), and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Links to other apps

The game contains links to apps by Mad Axe Studios, Veritas Studios and our friends on Google Play. Opening a link takes you to the Google Play Store, which is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network access: to load ads and consent forms.
  • Vibration: for optional haptic feedback (you can turn it off in Settings).
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

6. Children

Cold Call Chaos is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Security and retention

Because game data is stored only on your device, it is kept until you delete the game or clear its data. Information processed by Google AdMob is handled under Google's policies.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The effective date at the top will show the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: veritasstudios.support@gmail.com
