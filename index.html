Privacy Policy: Scam Call Simulator 3D

Developer: Mad Axe Studios · Effective date: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Scam Call Simulator 3D ("the game") handles, and why. We keep it simple: the game has no accounts and we do not run our own servers. The game uses a few Google services, described in sections 3 and 4.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or payment details. The game does not make real phone calls.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • Player name: the name you type when the boss asks is saved on your device so characters can greet you. It is never sent to us.
  • Game progress and settings: your career, upgrades, scores, notes you write in the in-game Notes app, and settings (sound, vibration, graphics) are saved locally.

This information stays on your phone. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, device and app information, IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google may use this to show personalized ads, where permitted and where you have consented.

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK, and some US states), the game asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before showing personalized ads.
  • You can change your ad choices at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices (when shown in your region), and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Analytics, crash reports and notifications (Google Firebase)

The game uses Google Firebase to understand how the game is played, fix crashes and deliver optional news. Google processes this information on our behalf. It is not linked to your name, and we do not sell it.

  • Firebase Analytics: how you use the game (for example sessions, screens, days started, calls finished, missions and upgrades), device and app information (device model, Android version, app version, language, approximate country derived from your IP address), an app-instance identifier and, where available, your device's advertising ID. We use this to improve the game and its balance.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: when the game crashes or hits an error, a crash report with the error details, device and app information, a crash-reporting installation identifier and basic game state (for example the current in-game day).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: a notification token so we can send occasional game news and event notifications. On Android 13 and later the game asks for permission first, and you can turn notifications off at any time in your Android settings.
  • Firebase Remote Config: a Firebase installation identifier used to download game settings (such as balance values) from Google's servers.

Learn more: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Links to other apps

The game contains links to apps by Mad Axe Studios, Veritas Studios and our friends on Google Play. Opening a link takes you to the Google Play Store, which is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

6. Permissions

  • Internet / network access: to load ads and consent forms, download game settings, and send analytics and crash reports.
  • Notifications: to show optional game news (Android 13 and later ask you first).
  • Vibration: for optional haptic feedback (you can turn it off in Settings).
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

7. Children

Scam Call Simulator 3D is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Security and retention

Because game data is stored only on your device, it is kept until you delete the game or clear its data. Information processed by Google AdMob and Google Firebase is sent over encrypted connections and kept under Google's retention policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The effective date at the top will show the latest version.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: veritasstudios.support@gmail.com
